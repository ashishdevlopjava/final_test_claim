FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY - NOT A REAL CONTRACT

Policy number: DEMO-HOME-001

Sudden and accidental direct physical loss to covered property may be eligible for
review, subject to the policy terms, limits, exclusions, deductible, and facts of
loss. Sudden discharge or overflow of water from a plumbing system may be covered
when the loss is not otherwise excluded. Long-term seepage, wear, and lack of
maintenance may be excluded; an adjuster must review the applicable wording and
the circumstances before reaching a conclusion.

The policyholder should take reasonable steps to prevent additional damage and
keep receipts for emergency mitigation. A claim submission should include the
date and description of loss, photographs of affected property, repair estimates
or invoices, and any relevant receipts. Additional information may be requested
by the assigned claims adjuster.
